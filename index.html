The Project Management Playbook

30 common pitfalls, 5,059 poll votes and practical insights from practice — a lifecycle-level look at why projects stall, from objective-setting to closure. Built from a 30-part LinkedIn series I designed, ran and analysed on my own.

How I apply it to delivery: my research points to closure as where value is lost: stakeholders disengage and no one owns the handover. I use the same lens on every project: clear objectives up front, named ownership, and a closure plan from day one.
